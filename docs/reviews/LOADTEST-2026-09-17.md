# prodcal scratch pipeline capacity results

Scope: shell-only reproduction in `scratch/loadtest`; no service requests, service changes, or DB access. `pandoc 3.11`; `typst 0.12.0 (737895d7)`; VM: 2 vCPU, 7,391 MiB RAM, no swap.

## Inputs

| Input | Size | Pandoc plain-text words | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `The-Twitter-Years-2026-04-16-0240.docx` | 6,811,707 B | 139,126 | large test manuscript |
| `scratch/smoke/ms.docx` | 6,811,707 B | 139,126 | byte-identical SHA-256 to the “large” file; it is not a distinct small input |

The PDF reproduction used the server's Pandoc command, header replacement, cleanup transforms, and the manuscript's three custom styles. EPUB used the server's default metadata/TOC options and the two CJK fallback fonts selected by the server's DOCX scan (34 CJK runes). No project DB/spec was read.

## A. Single full build (wall; peak RSS per child)

| Input | DOCX→Typst Pandoc | Typst→PDF | DOCX→EPUB Pandoc | Full pipeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| large | 4.00 s; 364,272 KiB | 0.93 s; 180,528 KiB | 3.84 s; 335,996 KiB | 8.84 s; 364,272 KiB |
| `ms.docx` | 3.71 s; 364,236 KiB | 1.10 s; 180,636 KiB | 3.87 s; 335,948 KiB | 8.77 s; 364,236 KiB |

## B. Full large-manuscript concurrency

Process-group RSS was sampled every 0.2 s; `MemAvailable` is the minimum observed. All builds exited 0. `dmesg` before/after had no delta and no OOM record.

| Parallel N | Total wall | Per-build wall | Max summed build RSS | Min MemAvailable |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 10.54 s | 10.54 s each | 719.0 MiB | 5,824 MiB |
| 4 | 23.55 s | 23.55 s each | 1,437.9 MiB | 5,289 MiB |
| 8 | 50.13 s | 50.12 s each | 2,857.3 MiB | 4,231 MiB |

At N=8, stages slowed to Pandoc→Typst 20.87–23.41 s, Typst 4.46–7.15 s, and EPUB Pandoc 20.73–21.86 s (versus 4.00/0.93/3.84 s singly): CPU is the limiting resource.

## C. Output/blob disk estimate

One final pair: PDF 493,485 B + EPUB 20,628,411 B = 21,121,896 B (20.14 MiB). 30 people × 3 builds = 90 pairs = 1,900,970,640 B (1,812.9 MiB / 1.770 GiB) of output blobs, before SQLite page/WAL overhead. This is small relative to 72 GiB free disk.

## D. Typst font-cache probe

With a fresh scratch `XDG_CACHE_HOME`, identical first/second Typst compiles were 0.99 s / 180,888 KiB and 0.98 s / 180,832 KiB. No meaningful warm-up was observed for this document.

## Recommendation (5 lines)

1. **Yes only if queued:** 2 vCPU / 7 GB handled N=8 without OOM, but 30 unbounded simultaneous builds are not safe or responsive.
2. **Add a global build semaphore of 2:** observed throughput implies about 15 × 10.54 s = 158 s for 30 builds, while preserving headroom for the web service.
3. **CPU first:** move to 4 vCPU if lower workshop queue time is needed; N=4 and N=8 showed severe CPU contention.
4. **RAM is adequate with that semaphore:** do not prioritize a RAM bump; without a gate, linear N=8 RSS extrapolates to about 10.7 GiB for N=30, so use at least 16 GB if intentionally allowing that burst.
5. **Disk is not the constraint:** budget roughly 1.8 GiB for the 90 stored output pairs, plus normal SQLite overhead.
