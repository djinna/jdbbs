A copyeditor keeps a **style sheet** while reading: serial comma or not, US
or UK spelling, how dialogue is punctuated, which words stay capitalised.
Your book has one, on the **Style sheet** link beside the Factory.

It is not the same as paragraph styles (Heading 1, Normal). Those shape how
the book **looks**. The style sheet shapes how it **reads**.

## It starts from ours

Your sheet begins as a copy of the studio's [house sheet](/stylesheet/), and
**every rule starts accepted**. If our defaults suit the book, do nothing.

First, say whether the book is **fiction or nonfiction** (or *not yet*).
That adds the rules for that kind of book. You can switch later and keep
everything you've edited.

## What you can do

For each rule:

- **Reject** it. It's marked *not for this book*.
- **Edit** the wording. It's marked *edited by you*.
- **Restore ours** to go back to the studio's wording.
- **Add a note** for your copyeditor.

And **+ Add a rule** to any section; your own rules are marked *your rule*.
**Accept all** puts back any rules you rejected and keeps your edits and
additions.

Changes save as you make them. **Download as Markdown** gives you the whole
sheet as a file for your copyeditor.
