**Why it's flagged.** Some text is highlighted, often a note to yourself or an unfinished bit of editing. Highlighting may not carry into the book, and the highlighted words may be something you meant to change.

**How serious.** Worth a look.

**How to fix it.** In any editor:

1. Read each highlighted passage and finish or delete it.
2. Remove the highlight ("no colour").
3. Upload again.

**When to leave it.** If the highlight means nothing now, you can leave it.
