Steps 5 and 6 on your Factory page. One build makes both files: the
**EPUB** and the **print PDF** of the interior at your trim size.

## Proof or final

| | Proof | Final |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Uses one of the finals on your pass |
| Print PDF | A small "PROOF" line at the foot of every page | Clean: the one you send your printer |
| EPUB | Identical either way | Identical either way |
| Limit | 30 per project in any 24 hours; the newest 5 are kept | 3 on a pass; more can be added |

Build proofs as often as you like while you revise. Read the **EPUB first**,
since it shows the text and structure fastest. Then read the PDF for pages and
breaks. Export a **final** when you're done.

A final that **fails isn't counted**. The final is refunded automatically. A
final that builds with a warning is counted, because the files are ready.

## How long

Usually a minute or two, longer if other books are building at the same
time. You can leave the page open. One build runs at a time per book.

## Downloads

Step 6 keeps everything:

- **Final files:** the print PDF and EPUB. Save copies somewhere of your
  own.
- **Latest proof:** the most recent proof PDF and EPUB.
- **Earlier builds:** every build, with its date and size.

Downloads keep working for 30 days after your pass ends. See
[Your Factory Pass](factory-pass.md).

## If a build fails

The page says what went wrong in plain words and, where it can, quotes the
text **near** the problem. Search for that phrase in your manuscript. Common
causes:

- **A style the factory doesn't know.** Declare it in the transmittal or
  change it. [Inspect](inspect.md) lists these first.
- **A chapter heading inside a styled block** (inside a quotation or
  code block, for example). Close the block before the heading.
- **An unreadable file.** Export it again from your editor as a `.docx`.

Stuck? Write to [j@djinna.com](mailto:j@djinna.com) with the message the
page showed.
