## Your client code

Every press or author has a **client code**, a short name like
`sample-press`. It's in your welcome email. Type it on the
[client portal](/portal) to reach your page, or go straight to
`studio.jdbb.net/sample-press/`.

"We don't recognize that client code" almost always means a small typo.

## Signing in

You sign in **by email**:

1. Type the address your Factory Pass (or welcome email) was sent to.
2. Choose **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and click the link.

The link works **once** and is good for **30 minutes**. Nothing after a few
minutes? Check your spam folder, and make sure you used the same address the
pass was sent to. The page won't tell you whether an address is on file.
That keeps other people from testing addresses.

You stay signed in on that browser for **90 days**.

**Have a password?** If your welcome email gave you one, choose *Have a
password? Use it instead*. Use the password from the email, not your client
code. If a link has expired or was already used, you'll see **Sign-in link
expired**. Choose **Request a new link**.

## Your books

Your page lists every project you have with the studio. Each card shows the
book's name, whether it runs on a Factory Pass, whether its transmittal is
draft or final, and two links:

- **Factory →** does the whole job: transmittal, upload, inspect, build and
  download. See [How the factory works](start-here.md).
- **Style sheet** holds your book's editorial decisions. See
  [Your style sheet](style-sheet.md).

**Recent activity** below the cards lists uploads, inspections, builds and
downloads as they happen.
