| 2026-09-27 | **Web3 payment option for the Factory Pass storefront.** The PI / Monstrous Times circle has Ethereum Foundation roots, so a wallet-native way to buy a pass is on-theme. Three tiers to weigh. **(a) Stripe “Crypto” payment method** — one Dashboard toggle once Stripe checkout exists (§STOREFRONT “after”): customer pays USDC (Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Solana, Tempo), settles to our balance in USD, 1.5% flat vs 2.9%+30¢ cards, refunds go back as stablecoin to the wallet, no dispute path, $10k cap, US-live (not NY). Zero blockchain code; the pass fulfils through the same `fulfillPass(source="stripe")` webhook. **(b) Coinbase Commerce / a self-custody USDC receive** — cheaper (~0.5–1%), settles on-chain, but we hold the reconciliation and tax burden; only worth it if buyers actually prefer paying a contract address. **(c) Pass-as-onchain-object** (Unlock Protocol–style lock, or a simple ERC-721 receipt on Base) — the *idea* fits the book (a Factory Pass is already a bearer token with a redeem code; “have your factory call my factory” between wallets), but it’s a product, not a payment method: wallet UX for authors who aren’t crypto-native, gas, and support forever for a one-person studio. **DIY paths skipped in the first draft (27 Sep, on Jenna’s pushback):** (d) a bare address — publish `jdbb.eth` / a Base address with the USDC price, a ~50-line watcher (Alchemy or Etherscan webhook) matches amount + order id and calls `fulfillPass(source="usdc")`; cost ≈ gas; we hold keys, reconciliation, manual refunds, tax basis — fine at pass volumes, and the most on-theme (factory-to-factory with no intermediary). (e) Daimo Pay (open-source, any-token-any-chain → USDC at our address, webhook) or Unlock Protocol if the pass itself should be the token. Bridge (Stripe-owned) also sells a plain USDC receive address that settles to bank. **Separate, smaller, better idea — colophon attestation:** anchor the final build’s colophon (sha256 of PDF/X + EPUB, transmittal id, Inspect counts, build time, fonts) as an **EAS attestation on Base**. No NFT, no wallet for the reader, pennies; a machine-checkable “this is the one” that beats the Human Authored seal and is the ISBN’s decentralised cousin — belongs in the book (ch. 16b/17) as well as the factory. Also look at **ISCC** (ISO 24138:2024), the content-derived identifier. **Recommendation:** (d) or (a) for payment when Stripe lands, as a checkbox — cost is near zero and it answers the community without committing us to anything on-chain. Note (c) as a possible workshop-2027 conversation with PI rather than a build.
