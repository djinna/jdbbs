1 · Transmittal

The transmittal is the record of how your book should be set — trim size, fonts, front matter, what to do with the odd bits. Fill it in first: when you mark it final, your Word template is generated from it, and the build follows it.

Open the transmittal → Download your Word template →

2 · Upload your manuscript

One Word file (.docx), written in the template generated from your transmittal (step 1). Upload a new one any time you revise — uploading is free.

Drop your Word file here, or choose a file
.docx only · up to 100 MB

3 · Inspect

Inspect — the preflight — reads your Word file and tells you what won’t come through the way you meant — stray styles, odd spacing, images that are too small to print. Unlimited — inspect as often as you like; it doesn’t use a build.

Open the full report →

What you get is a correctly typeset PDF and EPUB of the manuscript as it conforms to your transmittal. Inspect tells you what doesn’t conform.

4 · Build

One build makes both files: the EPUB and the typeset print PDF at your trim size. It takes a minute or two and uses one of your builds. You can stay on this page. When it lands, read the EPUB first — it’s the quickest way to see how the machine understood your file — then check the PDF, the one you send a printer.

A build that fails is not counted — you keep the credit.

5 · Download

Your files appear here after the first build.

Support
  • Live help during the workshop sessions (Sep 21–22) is included.
  • Outside that, email support is not included — the preflight report and the docs are the self-serve path. Live help: USD 100/hr, booked in advance, one-hour minimum.
Storage & expiry

Your pass keeps this project live for six months from the day it was set up. Until then you can upload revisions, inspect, spend any builds you have left, and download every build you’ve made.

After that date the project goes read-only: downloads keep working for another 30 days, then your manuscript and its builds are deleted and we keep only the record (title, dates, how many builds). Your unpublished book is yours — a hard expiry is the point, not an oversight. Keep your own copy of the files you care about.
