This is your book’s editorial style sheet: the decisions a copyeditor keeps while reading — serial comma or not, US or UK spelling, how dialogue is punctuated, which words stay capitalised. It is not the same thing as Word styles (Heading 1, Body Text), which are the named formats in the template we give you; those shape how the book looks, these shape how it reads.

We have seeded it from the studio house sheet, and every rule is already accepted. If our defaults suit the book, do nothing. Otherwise reject a rule, edit its wording, or add your own — your copyeditor works from what is here.
